# Architectural principles (intended)

**Status:** Stub. Target laws for Palm design. Refine with the vault.

1. **Intended over as-built** — this blueprint describes the shape we build toward.  
2. **One structure truth after load** — structure definition (and status under it) is structure law; profiles and env seed or package, they do not stay peer kings.  
3. **Materialize then admit** — decide what exists, wire only that, then gate business. Do not only refuse at the door.  
4. **Host wires once** — product and surfaces use published ports and admission, not the composition root as a bag.  
5. **Core purity** — `palm.core` imports nothing outside itself.  
6. **Registry extension** — new structure or product members prefer definition + register over private menus in hubs.  
7. **Layer direction** — surfaces depend on system; system does not depend on surfaces.  
8. **Job path stays business** — orchestration runs work; structure management does not become a second job engine.  
9. **Fail closed on readiness** — business that needs ground does not soft-open when structure is not ready.  
10. **Name residual** — temporary duals are debt, not architecture.
