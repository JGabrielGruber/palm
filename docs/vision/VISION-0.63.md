## 1. Goal

Give Palm a real **organism-ready** care between **boot** and **business**.

| Piece | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Assembly definition (DNA)** | Desired structure — single structure king after load |
| **Assembly engine** | Pure reconciler (definition + observations → status + effect intents) |
| **System loop + effect port** | Apply hands; household assemble |
| **Admission** | **One gate** — may business that needs ground run? Fail closed |
| **Builtin DNA seeds** | First decrees from shapes Palm already is (embedded first) |
| **Coherence suite** | Tests + guards whose job is **single truth**, not vanity green |

**Host** stays packaging and **seed chooser**.  
**Profiles / boot modes** shrink to **seed** of DNA — not parallel structure law.  
**Env (`PALM_*`)** seeds and packages; it does not stay structure king.  
**Planes** still start/continue business; citizens check admission.  
**Orchestration** still runs jobs. Assembly does not replace it.

### 1.1 Ambition and floor

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Floor** | Core assembly types + thin engine; system admission seat; **one** DNA path (embedded); **one** citizen path fail-closed; household can open the gate; coherence suite exists and **red is map** |
| **Growth line** | More DNA (cli, server, …); more citizens through the **same** gate; effect intents/handlers; profile→seed map; projection/invalidate; vitality/inspect present; reassemble on new definition; env structure pretenders purged |
| **Unplanned** | Named reserve slices for break inventory, dual-path discoveries, and work we cannot see yet — **not** shame; honesty |
| **Exit** | José judges readiness proper; ADR Accepted; residual kill-dated or paid; declared green bars hold without dual soft-ready |

Do not kill ambition to satisfy empty process.  
Do not ship permanent dual readiness “for CI.”  
Raise the wall; let pretenders break; swallow citizens.  
**Plan for the unplanned** — allocate room; do not invent dual paths when impact surprises.

### 1.2 Success (theme intent — not a kill checklist)

- A reader can point to **assembly** as reconciler between boot and business.  
- **Single readiness truth** via admission on owned paths.  
- **Citizens** only through the gate; **household** assembles without deadlock; **pretenders** purged or named residual.  
- First DNA is **embedded**; dogfood DNA (cli / server) follows as growth or early floor extension.  
- Env and CLI **seed** DNA and packaging; they do not compete as structure kings after load.  
- Coherence suite proves fail-closed and maps dual mode by **not working** under illegal conditions.  
- Docs, ADR, and debt match code. José closes when he feels it.

This theme is **not** full Grove mesh.  
This theme is **not** replace orchestration.  
This theme is **not** full surface compost.  
This theme is **not** multi-process CAS (SD-019) unless natural.

## 4. Principles

Bind to [PALM.md](../PALM.md), [VISION-ASSEMBLY](VISION-ASSEMBLY.md), [PHILOSOPHY.md](../../PHILOSOPHY.md), and ADR-032.

2. **One gate** — citizens fail closed; household is assemble, not a market-day skip.  
3. **Purge pretenders** — break, swallow, or kill-date residual.  
4. **DNA is structure king** after load; profiles/modes/env **seed**.  
5. **Full-in on the law, staged on the purge** — not half dual forever.  
6. **Embedded DNA first** — smallest decree; prove engine + gate.  
7. **Env packages and seeds** — storage, paths, ports, logs stay; structure toggles map into seed or die.  
8. **Coherence over vanity green** — suite exists to know truth; red maps dual mode.  
9. **Pure engine** — fake observations; no host/product imports in core.  
10. **Port-only clients** — product and planes take admission (+ domain ports), not control, not root.  
11. **Effect port ≠ ExecutionPort** — structure hands vs job effects.  
12. **Registry extension + seat DI** — boy-scout on touch (SD-016).  
13. **Plan for the unplanned** — reserve slices; impact unknown is allowed.  
14. **STE** for theme docs.  
15. **Exit is José’s judgment** when he feels readiness proper.

**Spirit:** A living kingdom that knows when it is not whole beats a green CI that lies.

## 6. DNA requirements (scope bar — freeze early)

Vision without shaped DNA delivers half. Requirements:

### 6.1 First DNA

**`local.embedded`** (name may lock in code) is the **first** structure decree.

- Grounded in today’s `CompositionProfile.embedded` / `BootMode.safe`+`test` body.  
- Core services; no server surfaces; no background drain membership as structure law.  
- Prove: load DNA → assemble household → admission under law → citizen fail-closed before ready.

`safe` / `test` are largely **household/policy** on that body (recover, log, background off) — not three structure DNAs unless a real structure split appears.

### 6.2 Early dogfood DNA (growth or early extension)

| DNA (draft id) | Entry | Notes |
|----------------|-------|--------|

### 6.3 Env and seed

| Env / packaging | Role under 0.63 |
|-----------------|-----------------|

| Structure toggles that override composition as peer law | **Pretenders** — map into seed or purge |

Spawner (later): place body + hand decree. Child DNA shapes; spawner does not hand-assemble menus forever.

### 6.4 Ready meaning (v0)

For embedded (and early locals): **definition-ready** means local ground/projection/seed law for that DNA holds — **no remote authority required on floor**. Place-book heavy DNA is growth.

## 7. Coherence suite (truth instrument)

**Goal is not vanity green.** Goal is to know whether single truth holds.

| Kind | Job |
|------|-----|
| **Pure engine tests** | Definition, status, tick, observations — no host |
| **Negative / fail-closed tests** | Citizen work with admission down **must not** run |
| **Integration** | Only assemble path makes admission true for the DNA under test |
| **Structural guards** | Core purity; no product reassemble; no second admission king in graph (ratchet) |
| **Break inventory** | When gate rises, red list = pretender map |

Early red after the wall is **information**. Soft-flagging suite green is dual mode.  
Wrong tests that start citizens with admission down: **fix or delete**.

Home (guide): `tests/assembly/` (or equivalent) + `just guard-*` extensions when homes exist. Exact paths lock in code slices.
